# Privacy Policy for Muffler

Muffler is a fully open-source Chrome extension designed to automatically manage audio focus workspaces by balancing background tab volumes. 

### Data Collection & Privacy
* **No Data Collection:** Muffler does not collect, store, track, monitor, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or user metrics.
* **100% Local Execution:** All configuration settings (such as your preferred ducking volume) are stored locally on your device via the standard `chrome.storage` API. 
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or processed by third parties.

If you have any questions or would like to audit the source code, please review the repository files or open an issue directly on GitHub.
